Unoyama Holdings [LEGAL ENTITY SUFFIX — confirm] ("Unoyama Holdings", "we", "us") is a private holding company. This policy explains, in plain language, how we treat information. It is short because our situation is simple: we hold interests in businesses, we publish a small informational website, and we do not offer products or services to the public.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We have no customers, end users, or account holders, and we operate no consumer product.
  • Any software we build is for our own internal business use.
  • This website is static: no accounts, no cookies set by us, no trackers.
  • We handle the limited information we do hold in line with applicable law.

Overview

Unoyama Holdings is a holding company. It does not operate a public application, marketplace listing, or subscription service, and it does not maintain customer records, user accounts, or consumer profiles. There is no product through which a member of the public provides information to us.

This policy covers the two narrow contexts in which information reaches us at all: our public website, and correspondence someone chooses to send us.

What Information We Manage

Information you send us directly. If you contact us by email or other correspondence, we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, your email address, and the contents of your message. We do not require you to provide anything, and we ask that you not send sensitive personal information.

Information related to our website. The website is a set of static pages. We set no cookies, run no scripts, load no external assets, and include no analytics or trackers. We do not collect anything from visitors ourselves. Our hosting provider may record standard server-log information — such as IP address, browser user agent, and requested URL — as an ordinary part of delivering the pages. Those logs belong to the provider under its own privacy terms; we do not receive, request, or use them.

Ordinary business records. Like any company, we hold routine records about our own personnel, advisors, vendors, and counterparties — for example contact details and contract, corporate, and accounting records. This information comes from those relationships, not from the public, and is used only to run the company.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • respond to correspondence someone sends us;
  • administer the company's own corporate, financial, and tax affairs;
  • manage relationships with our personnel, advisors, vendors, and counterparties;
  • maintain the security and integrity of our own systems;
  • meet legal, regulatory, accounting, and record-keeping obligations.

Information is never used for:

  • selling, renting, or trading to anyone;
  • advertising, behavioral profiling, or marketing to consumers;
  • training machine-learning or AI models;
  • any purpose unrelated to operating Unoyama Holdings.

Who Can Access Your Information

Access is limited to:

  • authorized Unoyama Holdings personnel who need it for their work;
  • professional advisors acting on our behalf, such as our accountants, auditors, tax preparers, and counsel;
  • service providers that support our own internal operations, bound to handle information only for us;
  • authorities, where disclosure is required by law or legal process.

Information is never shared with advertisers, data brokers, marketing networks, or any party seeking it for its own purposes. We do not disclose information as part of any commercial arrangement.

How We Protect Your Information

Technical safeguards. This website is served over HTTPS. Company systems are access-controlled, and credentials are held in a password manager rather than in shared files or source control.

Organizational safeguards. Access is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis and is reviewed when roles change. Access is revoked when it is no longer required.

Internal software. Any software Unoyama Holdings builds is for its own internal use, is run by authorized personnel on company-controlled systems, and is not distributed to or usable by anyone else. Where such software connects to a third-party service, it does so under credentials the company itself authorizes. See the End-User License Agreement.

No system is perfectly secure, and this policy does not guarantee that these measures will prevent every possible incident.

Your Privacy Rights

Our commitments. We retain information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as our legal, tax, and corporate record-keeping obligations require, and we dispose of it afterwards. If you have corresponded with us and would like that correspondence deleted, tell us and we will honor the request unless we are required to keep it.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to object to how it is used. Because we hold very little — generally only correspondence and ordinary business records — these requests are usually straightforward. Contact us at the address below and we will respond as applicable law requires. We will not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.
