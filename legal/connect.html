Connecting and reconnecting

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Unoyama Holdings publishes no public applications. Any software it builds is for internal use by its own personnel, so there is no public sign-up or connection flow on this website.

Where an internal tool connects to a third-party service, an authorized person at Unoyama Holdings starts that connection from inside the service, using credentials the company holds. To ask about an existing connection, or to reconnect one, write to legal@unoyama.com.

See also Disconnecting, the Privacy Policy, and the End-User License Agreement.
